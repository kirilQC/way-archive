The Cost of Following

Sunday, September 6, 2026 · Pastor Daniel Reyes · 42 min
▶  Embedded YouTube player

Summary

Jesus turns to the large crowds following Him and delivers one of His hardest teachings: no one builds a tower without first counting the cost. This sermon walks through Luke 14, arguing that casual Christianity is a contradiction — following Christ reorders our loyalties to family, possessions, and even our own lives. The message closes with three practical costs to count this week: time, treasure, and reputation.

Highlights

Discipleship is not an add-on to life — it is a reordering of it.
"The tower half-built is the testimony half-told."
Three costs to count: time, treasure, reputation.

Key Scriptures

Luke 14:28"For which of you, desiring to build a tower, does not first sit down and count the cost…"
