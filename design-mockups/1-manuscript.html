The Cost of Following

Sunday, September 6, 2026 · Pastor Daniel Reyes · 42 min
▶  EMBEDDED YOUTUBE PLAYER

Summary

Jesus turns to the large crowds following Him and delivers one of His hardest teachings: no one builds a tower without first counting the cost. This sermon walks through Luke 14, arguing that casual Christianity is a contradiction — that following Christ reorders our loyalties to family, possessions, and even our own lives. The pastor closes with three practical costs the congregation is invited to count this week.

Key Scriptures

Luke 14:28"For which of you, desiring to build a tower, does not first sit down and count the cost…"
Luke 9:23"If anyone would come after me, let him deny himself…"

Highlights

Discipleship is not a add-on to life — it is a reordering of it.
"The tower half-built is the testimony half-told."
Three costs to count: time, treasure, reputation.
