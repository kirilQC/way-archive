You asked

Are Jewish people saved?

Scripture is clear that salvation comes through faith in Jesus for everyone, Jew and Gentile alike. Paul, himself a Jew, wrote that there is no distinction: all have sinned, and all are made right with God freely through Christ.

Romans 3:22-24 "We are made right with God by placing our faith in Jesus Christ. And this is true for everyone who believes, no matter who we are." (NLT)

At the same time, Paul agonized over his own people and insisted God has not rejected them. Israel's story is not finished; God's covenant faithfulness remains, and Paul's deepest hope was that his kinsmen would come to know their Messiah.

Romans 11:1 "I ask, then, has God rejected his own people, the nation of Israel? Of course not!" (NLT)

So the answer is not about ethnicity but about faith. Being Jewish neither saves automatically nor disqualifies. The invitation of the gospel stands open, and the New Testament ends Romans 9 through 11 with worship at the depth of God's mercy toward all.
