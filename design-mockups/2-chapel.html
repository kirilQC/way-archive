The Cost of Following

Sunday, September 6, 2026 · 42 min · Luke 14:25–33
▶  Embedded YouTube player

AI Summary

Jesus turns to the large crowds following Him and delivers one of His hardest teachings: no one builds a tower without first counting the cost. This sermon walks through Luke 14, arguing that casual Christianity is a contradiction — following Christ reorders our loyalties to family, possessions, and even our own lives. The message closes with three practical costs the congregation is invited to count this week: time, treasure, and reputation.

Highlights & Notes

1
Discipleship is not an add-on to life — it is a reordering of it.
2
"The tower half-built is the testimony half-told."
3
Three costs to count this week: time, treasure, reputation.
