What has been preached on anxiety?

Way Church keeps coming back to one idea when it talks about anxiety: anxious thoughts lose their grip when you fix your mind on the character of God. Noah Herrin teaches from Hebrews 13:7-9 that because Jesus is the same yesterday, today, and forever, you never have to fill the unknown with worst-case scenarios.

Practically, the church points to prayer as the exchange rate for worry. Instead of rehearsing the problem, you tell God what you need and thank him for what he has already done Philippians 4:6-7. Grant Troutt adds that peace is developed inside hardship, not by escaping it, using David's years in the cave as the backdrop.

The invitation in every one of these messages is the same: give God the worry itself, not just the tidy version of it, because he genuinely cares about you 1 Peter 5:7.

Cited in this answer

1 Peter 5:7"Give all your worries and cares to God, for he cares about you." (NLT)
